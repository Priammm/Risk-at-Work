Risk At Work

A hazard is anything that may cause harm, such as a wet floor or a chemical. A risk is the chance, high or low, that someone will be harmed by the hazard, together with how serious the harm could be. In the UK, employers must assess the risks in their workplace and take reasonable steps to control them under the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 and the Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999.
